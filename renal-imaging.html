Clinical implementation notes

RENAL describes anatomical complexity, not malignancy probability. Bosniak v2019 is an imaging classification, not a numeric malignancy-risk score. UISS combines stage, Fuhrman grade and ECOG performance status. The original SSIGN score must not be confused with the related Leibovich 2003 recurrence model.
