Methods, evidence and limitations

The seminoma explorer uses rounded multivariable hazard ratios reported by Wagner et al.: LDH 1.67, β-hCG 1.89, lymphovascular invasion 1.82, rete testis invasion 1.81 and hilar soft tissue invasion 2.83. Rete and hilar soft tissue invasion are alternative categories.

The illustrative calculation assumes a 6% five-year baseline risk and uses 1 − (1 − 0.06)product of hazard ratios. This does not reproduce the fitted Cox model, its confidence intervals or validated individual predictions. Do not interpret the result as a clinical risk estimate.

The original nationwide Danish study included 924 clinical stage I seminoma patients undergoing surveillance. Internal validation was reported; external validation was not reported in the article. Five-year relapse is distinct from lifetime relapse or cancer-specific mortality. These findings do not themselves establish an indication for adjuvant therapy.

Original research: Wagner, T. et al. (2024). Prognostic Factors for Relapse in Patients With Clinical Stage I Testicular Seminoma: A Nationwide, Population-Based Cohort Study. Journal of Clinical Oncology, 42(1), 81–89. doi:10.1200/JCO.23.00959.
