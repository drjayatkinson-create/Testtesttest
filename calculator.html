Stage I relapse
risk explorer.

Investigate published prognostic factors for relapse following orchidectomy and surveillance. For education and research only.

Not a clinical decision-support device. Seminoma estimates are approximate reconstructions, not exact published predictions. The non-seminoma model is awaiting verified coefficients and calibration. Do not use for patient management.

Pre-orchidectomy markers; histology from orchidectomy specimen.

Methodology & limitations

The seminoma prototype uses the multivariable hazard ratios published by Wagner et al.: LDH 1.67, β-hCG 1.89, lymphovascular invasion 1.82, rete invasion only 1.81, and hilar soft tissue invasion 2.83. The two hilum levels are alternative categories.

To illustrate relative risk combinations, the software assumes a 6% five-year risk without any factors and calculates 1 − (1 − 0.06)product of hazard ratios. This is an approximation. Rounded published hazard ratios and a rounded baseline risk do not reproduce the full fitted Cox model or its confidence intervals. It must not be interpreted as an individualised, clinically validated prediction.

The original Danish study included 924 clinical stage I seminoma patients managed with surveillance; the model was internally validated but not externally validated in the article. A 5-year prediction is not equivalent to lifetime risk or cancer-specific mortality. The model does not establish that adjuvant treatment is indicated.

Original model: Wagner, T. et al. (2024). Prognostic Factors for Relapse in Patients With Clinical Stage I Testicular Seminoma: A Nationwide, Population-Based Cohort Study. Journal of Clinical Oncology, 42(1), 81–89. doi:10.1200/JCO.23.00959.
